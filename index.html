1. Information we collect

a) Information you provide

  • Account data: Email, name, and a unique account ID (only if you create an account).
  • Financial data: Transactions, accounts, and categories you create.
  • Sign-in method: You can sign in with email and password or with your Google account. With Google sign-in, we receive your name and email; we never see your Google password.
  • Budgets and recurring rules: Stored only on your device and never sent to our cloud.
  • Preferences: Currency, theme, and language (stored locally on your device only).

b) Automatically collected data

  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregated usage events — no transaction titles or individual amounts.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash diagnostics — no financial data.
  • Google technical data: Device model, Android version, IP — managed by Google under their own policies.

2. How we use your information

  • Display and sync your financial data.
  • Generate AI-powered insights (section 5).
  • Improve app stability and experience.
  • Serve ads via AdMob (free version, with regional consent).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, commercial profiling, or to train our own AI models.

3. Data storage

  • On your device: the app works fully offline and without an account.
  • In the cloud (Google Cloud Firestore): only if you create an account and sign in, in a private space linked to your account.

Only you can read and change your cloud data. Security rules block everyone else, and Google's security checks block fake or modified versions of the app.

5. AI-powered insights

⚠️ Important: AI insights from BudgetX are for informational purposes only and do not constitute financial, tax, or investment advice. Consult a qualified financial professional for important decisions.

BudgetX uses Firebase AI (Google Gemini API) to generate budget insights. We only send aggregated financial data (category summaries, totals, and date ranges) — never the free-text titles of your transactions. Responses are saved in your private cloud space and are subject to a daily request cap.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of BudgetX shows banner and opt-in rewarded ads via Google AdMob. The app is configured as not directed at children.

Consent for EEA / UK / Switzerland

In those regions, we request explicit consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form, in accordance with GDPR / UK GDPR / Swiss nFADP. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown; app access is not affected.

Ad controls

  • Google Settings → Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws: Settings → Legal → Privacy preferences in the app to change your choice.
  • Purchase "Remove Ads" to permanently remove advertising.

7. In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed entirely by Google Play Billing. BudgetX does not receive or store any payment data. The purchase is automatically restored when you reinstall the app with the same Google Play account.

8. App permissions

BudgetX never asks you for a permission: none of the permissions below needs your approval. The app itself needs only two:

  • Internet access (INTERNET): to sync your data, show ads and load currency information.
  • Network status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to detect when you're back online and finish pending syncs.

The Google services the app uses add these automatically:

  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and related ad permissions): used by AdMob and Firebase Analytics to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, only as your consent choice allows (section 6). You can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings.
  • In-app purchases (BILLING): lets Google Play process the "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Install source (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): tells Firebase Analytics how the app was installed, for example from the Play Store.
  • Background tasks (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): let tasks such as syncing finish reliably.
  • Sign-in security check (READ_GSERVICES): used by Google's protection against abuse when you sign in.
  • Fingerprint and biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): added by Android's sign-in system for Google sign-in. BudgetX never uses your fingerprint or other biometric data.

We do not request access to your storage, camera, location, contacts, microphone or notifications. CSV export uses Android's file picker, which needs no permission.

17. How to delete your account and data

You can delete your BudgetX account and its data at any time, free of charge.

a) In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Settings and go to the Data section.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. For your security, enter your password or sign in again with Google.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and the data stored in our cloud: your profile (name, email and currency), transactions, accounts, categories, saved AI insights and AI usage records. It also erases the transactions, accounts, categories, budgets and recurring rules on that device. This cannot be undone.

b) Without the app (by email)

If you can't use the app, email mybudgetx@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete account". We will send a confirmation request to that address and, once you confirm, delete your account and cloud data within 30 days and let you know when it's done. We will never ask for your password.

c) If you use the app without an account

Your data exists only on your device. To erase it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

What is not deleted

  • Local data on other devices where you used BudgetX (uninstall the app or clear its data on each one).
  • CSV files you exported yourself.
  • Your "Remove Ads" purchase, which belongs to your Google Play account.
  • Basic app preferences (such as theme and your privacy consent choice), which stay on the device until you uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data and crash reports, which are not linked to your account and are kept according to Google's retention periods (section 11). Information previously sent to Google's AI service is handled under Google's terms.
